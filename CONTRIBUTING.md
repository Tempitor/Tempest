# Contributing to Tempest

Thanks for wanting to help. Tempest is published under the GNU GPL v3, and the
author also offers it to radio makers under separate license terms (see
[NOTICE](NOTICE)). To keep that second option possible, every contribution
needs the grant below.

2. **GPL for everyone.** Your contribution is licensed to everyone under the

3. **Relicensing for the author.** You also grant Tempitor (the project

   distribute your contribution under any license terms, including

4. **You keep your copyright.** This is a license, not a transfer. You can
   still use your own contribution however you like.
5. **No warranty.** Your contribution is provided "as is", without
   warranties of any kind.

## How to agree

Add a `Signed-off-by` line to every commit (`git commit -s` does this):

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>

The sign-off means you agree to the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1
(https://developercertificate.org/) **and** to the contributor license grant
above. Pull requests with unsigned commits can't be merged.

## Ground rules

- Never commit stock firmware, full patched `.bin` files, or bytes copied
  from manufacturer firmware. Mods are shipped as patches only.
- Keep changes focused, and describe what you tested on real hardware.
- Transmit only within your license and local rules.
